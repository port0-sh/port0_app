About port0.sh

A constant-product automated market maker running on the Miden testnet. Pools, swaps, liquidity, and the token launchpad are all real on-chain accounts and notes; the tokens and the network are not.

What it is

port0.sh is an XYK (constant-product) AMM. A pool holds two fungible assets and enforces the invariant x · y = k between their reserves. A swap adds one asset and removes the other at the price the product implies, minus a fee that accrues to liquidity providers and, at the registry's rate, to the protocol account.

Each pool is a single public Miden account. Its logic lives in an account component, its state lives in account storage, and every operation arrives as a network note the node consumes on the pool's behalf. There is no off-chain order book and no server that holds your keys.

How a pool works

Reserves
The balances of the two pool assets, held in the pool account vault.
LP supply
The pool issues its own LP token as a native fungible asset. LPs hold it in proportion to their share of the reserves.
LP fee
Chosen per pool from three tiers: 0.05%, 0.30%, 1.00%. Paid by every swap to LPs.
Protocol fee
A share of the LP fee, fixed by the registry and sent to the protocol account. The swapper pays the LP fee tier and nothing more.

Add liquidity by depositing both assets; the pool mints LP tokens at the current ratio. Remove liquidity by burning LP tokens for your proportional share, including accrued swap fees.

Fees and network costs

Every transaction on Miden pays a network fee in the chain's native fee asset. You must hold it in your wallet before you swap, deposit, withdraw, or create a pool. Network-note operations pay the pool its note-consumption fee through a sponsorship note that the app attaches automatically.
