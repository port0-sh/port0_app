0. Launch a token (optional)

Mints a fixed-supply token straight to your wallet: the faucet is created with maxSupply equal to the initial supply and the whole supply is minted to you. Nobody can mint more. Your wallet first sends 0.000420 USDCX to the new faucet: exactly the two transaction fees (consume + mint, 2 × 210 base units) the faucet will pay.
